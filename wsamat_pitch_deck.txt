# WSAMat Personal Safety Hub Pitch Deck\n\nSlide 1: Title\nWSAMat Personal Safety Hub\nSmart, Silent & Secure Emergency Protection\n\nSlide 2: Problem\nIn emergency situations, unlocking a phone and making a call is too slow and dangerous. Victims need a way to instantly notify authorities and contacts covertly.\n\nSlide 3: Solution\nWSAMat allows 1-tap or shake-to-activate emergency SOS. It silently records audio, captures photos, and sends live GPS tracking directly to trusted contacts and emergency services.\n\nSlide 4: Key Features\n- 1-Tap & Shake SOS\n- Covert Audio & Photo Capture\n- Live GPS Tracking via SMS/WhatsApp\n- Disguised App Icons (Calculator, Clock)\n- Automated 112 Dialing\n\nSlide 5: Technology Stack\n- Kotlin / Java\n- SQLite Local Secure Database\n- Camera2 API & AudioRecord API\n- Android Fused Location Provider
